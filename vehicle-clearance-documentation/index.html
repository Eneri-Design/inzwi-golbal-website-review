Vehicle Clearance & Documentation

Export permits, clearance certificates and customs documentation, processed by a registered South African vehicle exporter.

Export paperwork, handled for you

Without professional help, export paperwork can be slow and complicated. We streamline applications and reduce delays so you can focus on the purchase. Requirements vary by destination country, and our team guides you through the specific requirements for yours.

What we assist with

  • Vehicle export clearance permits
  • ITAC applications where applicable
  • Vehicle clearance certificates
  • Customs documentation
  • Border compliance documentation
  • Export registration assistance

What is a Vehicle Export Clearance Certificate?

It is an official document required to legally export a vehicle from South Africa. It confirms the vehicle is approved for export and complies with the regulations.
