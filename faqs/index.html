Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about sourcing and exporting a vehicle from South Africa.

Can I import a vehicle into my country from South Africa?

Yes. We assist customers across Africa and beyond with sourcing, export documentation and clearance, including Zimbabwe, Zambia, Botswana, Mozambique, Namibia, Malawi, the DRC and many others.

Do you sell both new and pre-owned vehicles?

Yes. We source brand-new and quality pre-owned vehicles from reputable dealerships, manufacturers, fleet operators and trusted suppliers across South Africa.

Can you source a specific vehicle model for me?

Absolutely. Give us the make, model, year, specification or budget and our sourcing team will search our network for the best options.

How do I purchase a vehicle through Inzwi Global?

Contact us with your requirements. We source options, send quotations and vehicle details, assist with export documentation and guide you from start to finish.

What documents are required to export a vehicle?

Requirements vary by destination country. Our team will guide you through the specific requirements for yours.

How long does export documentation take?

Export documentation typically takes 2–5 working days.

What is a Vehicle Export Clearance Certificate?

An official document required to legally export a vehicle from South Africa. It confirms the vehicle is approved for export and complies with the regulations.

Do you assist with ITAC export permits?

Yes. We assist with applications and documentation for vehicle export permits and compliance requirements where applicable.

Can you arrange delivery to the border?

Yes. We can assist with transport and delivery to designated border posts or export points within South Africa.

Can I buy a vehicle without visiting South Africa?

Yes. Many clients buy remotely. We provide details, photographs, specifications and quotations so you can decide with confidence.

Do you inspect vehicles before export?

Yes. We strive to source quality vehicles and can provide additional vehicle information and inspection details where available.

What vehicle brands do you supply?

A wide range of leading brands including Toyota, Volkswagen, BMW, Nissan, Honda, Isuzu, Lexus, Haval, Hino and many more.

Can businesses purchase multiple vehicles or fleets?

Yes. We work with businesses, NGOs, government departments, transport operators and fleet owners who need multiple vehicles.

Do you supply trucks and commercial vehicles?

Yes. Besides passenger vehicles we source trucks, bakkies and pickups, SUVs, commercial vans, fleet vehicles and heavy-duty vehicles.

Do you supply automotive spare parts?

Yes. We source quality parts and accessories for a wide range of makes and models.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment options are discussed during the quotation. We give secure payment instructions once a vehicle has been selected and approved.

Can you help me choose the right vehicle?

Certainly. We recommend vehicles based on your budget, intended use, fuel economy needs, passenger capacity and destination country.

Why choose Inzwi Global?

We are a registered South African vehicle exporter with professional sourcing, export documentation help, reliable support, competitive pricing, access to leading brands, parts supply and extensive industry experience.

What does it mean when Inzwi Global facilitates a vehicle purchase?

If you have found a vehicle at a dealership, auction or private seller, we help negotiate the price, verify its history, inspect its condition and make sure it meets your expectations before you buy.

How long from payment to the vehicle being ready for delivery?

Once payment is confirmed, vehicle preparation and documentation typically take 7–10 working days. This can vary with the vehicle, documentation and destination regulations.

Can you source a vehicle on my behalf?

Yes. We find vehicles to your make, model, year, budget and specification. If you have already found one, we can inspect it and assist with purchase, export documentation and delivery.
