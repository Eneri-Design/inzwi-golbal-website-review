About Inzwi Global

A registered South African vehicle exporter built on integrity, professionalism and reliability.

Who we are

Inzwi Global is a registered South African vehicle export company supplying quality vehicles and automotive solutions across Southern Africa. We serve individuals, businesses, government institutions, NGOs and fleet operators, with compliance with export regulations and customs requirements built in.

Our goal is to be Africa’s preferred vehicle sourcing and export partner. The company was founded by Lisah Tais Mwenda, Founder & Managing Director.

Mission

High-quality vehicles that are safe, reliable and competitively priced, with exceptional service through the purchase and ownership journey.

Vision

A future where transport is accessible, efficient and dependable for people and businesses across Africa.
