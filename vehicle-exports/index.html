Vehicle Sourcing & Sales

New and quality pre-owned vehicles from reputable South African dealerships and fleet suppliers, sourced for export to your country.

New and pre-owned vehicles, sourced for you

We source new and quality pre-owned vehicles from reputable dealerships, manufacturers, fleet operators and trusted suppliers across South Africa. Give us the make, model, year, specification or budget and our team searches its network for the best options.

Many clients buy remotely. We send details, photographs, specifications and quotations so you can decide with confidence, without travelling to South Africa.

What we supply

  • Passenger vehicles and SUVs
  • Bakkies and double cab pickups
  • Commercial vans
  • Trucks and heavy-duty vehicles
  • Fleet vehicles for businesses, NGOs and government departments

Brands include Toyota, Volkswagen, BMW, Nissan, Honda, Isuzu, Lexus, Haval, Hino and many more.

Found a vehicle already?

If you have found a vehicle at a dealership, auction or private seller, we help negotiate the price, verify its history, inspect its condition and make sure it meets your expectations before you buy. Then we handle the purchase, export documents, clearance and delivery.
